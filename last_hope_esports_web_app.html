TOURNAMENT RULES & GENERAL CODE OF CONDUCT

All players must strictly comply with these rules. Violations will result in warnings or immediate permanent ban.

General Match Rules

  • Identity Verification: Players must use their registered in-game ID and Discord tag provided in their profile settings.
  • Room Credentials Security: Custom Room Passwords are strict confidential data. Sharing credentials to unverified players results in an immediate ban.
  • Punctuality: Teams must join the match lobby 15 minutes prior to the designated match start time.
  • Proof Submission: Team captains must take screenshots/recordings of victory screens for result verification.

Cheating & Punishments Policy

  • Zero Tolerance Cheating: Use of third-party software, scripts, exploit bugs, or hacks results in immediate permanent hardware ban.
  • Toxic Behavior: Toxicity towards opponents or tournament admins will yield strikes / warnings. 3 strikes lead to a season ban.
  • Public Warning Log: Infractions, warnings, and bans are publicly logged in the Public Bans tab.
